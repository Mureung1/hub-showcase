Market Sentiment

The Federal Reserve is expected to maintain current interest rates despite inflationary pressures.

연준은 인플레이션 압력에도 불구하고 현재 금리를 유지할 것으로 예상됩니다.

In a financial context, maintain means to keep something at its current level without change.

Financial Tip:

"Interest rates" are the cost of borrowing money. Maintaining them usually signals a "wait-and-see" approach from the central bank.

"Despite" is followed by a noun or noun phrase. In this sentence, it's followed by "inflationary pressures".
